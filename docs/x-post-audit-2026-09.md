## Recurring flyer template patterns observed

- **Webinar/workshop flyer** — WVF logo + partner logo header, bold
  all-caps title, bullet list of "what you'll learn" or "key benefits,"
  speaker headshot + bio block, event-details box (date/time/location/
  cost), QR code + "Register Now" CTA, social icons + contact footer
  (www.womensventurefund.org / 212-563-0499).
- **Quote/motivational card** — Simple typographic card (hand-lettered
  or clean sans-serif) with an empowerment quote, no logistics — used
  to fill in between promotional pushes.
- **Community/partner event flyer** — Co-branded with an outside
  partner (Senator Cordell Cleare, SCORE NYC, City Bar Justice Center,
  Chase), heavier on sponsor logos and photo collages, still keeps the
  date/time/location/QR-code block.
- **Gala/experience flyer** (Harlem Legacy Experience) — More
  editorial/luxury styling, honoree photo, sponsor logo wall, ticketing
  via Eventbrite rather than Zoom.
- **Stock-photo posts** — Used for softer CTAs (loan inquiry, "Money
  Matters" teaser) where the caption carries all the copy and the image
  is just a lifestyle photo of women entrepreneurs.
